# eb-tfhers: the error bootstrap on TFHE-rs 1.8.1

The error bootstrap of the paper (case (B): p = 2N, N = 2048, one ciphertext at a time) built on the primitives of
TFHE-rs, so that it can be compared with TFHE-rs itself with the same FFT, the same external product and the same keys.

* From TFHE-rs: binary GLWE key, bootstrapping keys (classic or multi-bit) and their Fourier conversion, the blind
  rotation (`blind_rotate_assign`, `multi_bit_blind_rotate_assign`, fed with our own exponents through the public traits
  `ModulusSwitchedLweCiphertext` / `MultiBitModulusSwitchedLweCiphertext`), sample extraction, LWE arithmetic.
* Ours (ported from `julia/src`): the polynomial stair key switch (`src/stair.rs`, d = 16), the rounding at the
  resolution R = p̂·2N coordinate by coordinate with recentering and selection (`src/rounding.rs`), the test
  polynomial E ↦ E/R and the subtraction (`src/eb.rs`).

Rotation: m = 1, the CMux of TFHE-rs (`blind_rotate_assign`), or m = 2, our multi-bit rotation on its FFT (`eb::Own2`:
for each pair of coordinates, one decomposition and 2ℓ forward FFTs, the products by the GGSW of the indicators of
the patterns 10, 01, 11 (generated by TFHE-rs as the bootstrapping key of the vector of indicators), the factors
X^e - 1 applied in the Fourier domain, 2 backward FFTs; the fresh errors are doubled, the error of the FFT is
2^-84.5 B^2 ℓ per pair). On the Apple M3 Max it is 1.12 to 1.21 times faster than the CMux for ℓ = 1 to 4 (`rot_ab`);
the multi-bit rotation of TFHE-rs (`TFHEMB=1`) is slower on one thread and noisier.

Configurations (`src/params.rs`): `CONFIGS_RS2` (default), the cheaper of m = 1 and m = 2 for each k
(chosen by the noise model with the time models of the Mac:
m = 2 except at k = 2 and 7); `CFG=rs`: `CONFIGS_RS`, m = 1, chosen for the error of the FFT of TFHE-rs,
2^-87 B^2 ℓ per external product (calibrated by `eb_fft`); `CFG=jl`: the configurations of the Julia prototype
(m = 2, error of FFTW).

## Binaries

    cargo run --release --bin eb_noise     # noise of the refresh and of the key switch, margins (k = 1..8)
    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin eb_curves   # times, one thread: error bootstrap vs TFHE-rs
    cargo run --release --bin eb_fft       # calibration of the error of the FFT of TFHE-rs (m = 1 and 2)
    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin rot_ab      # A/B: CMux of TFHE-rs / our multi-bit rotation (m = 2)

`eb_curves` prints, in the same session: the whole refresh of the error bootstrap for k = 1..8 (p̂ = 4^k), the
refresh of `FheUint2k` by TFHE-rs with its default parameters (`&a & MAX`, high-level API), and one key switch + PBS
of a 2_2 block by TFHE-rs (core_crypto, one thread), classic and multi-bit. Environment: `KS=1,4` (some k only),
`REPS` (default 10), `CFG=jl`, `EBM=1,2`.

## CombBoot (`src/comb.rs`)

Algorithm 1 of the paper, case (B) (p = 2^β, M0 = B = 2N/p columns, h = 1), with the rotation of TFHE-rs's classic
form (m = 1: one CMux per mask coordinate, `cmux_assign_mem_optimized`), ported from `julia/src/algorithm.jl`. The
Fourier values of TFHE-rs are reordered as in the T-layout of `julia/src/fast_opt.jl`: index e = κ + L m for the root
ψ^{5^e} (L = p/4, m < B; the roots at which TFHE-rs evaluates are read on the transform of X). Then the B - 1
automorphisms τ_j: X ↦ X^{γ^j} (γ = 1 + p) are rotations of B blocks of L values, and the public polynomials Q_j are
L values repeated on the B blocks (half table of B-point DFTs). The key switchings share one decomposition (hoisting,
shifted gadget, carry-free digits) and are multiplied by Q_j in the same pass, block by block, with FMA. On the cloud VM
at 8 bits, a CombBoot went from 281 to about 155 ms per call (outputs bit for bit identical before the FMA).
Since 2026-10-16 (`comb::KS_MODE`, `comb::KS_PAD`): the key switchings run source block by source block (the digits
and the FFT of b of a block read once for the B - 1 automorphisms, the keys stored source-major; `KS_MODE = 0`: by
automorphism, as before), and their arrays (digits, FFT of C0, accumulators, Q_j, keys) sit in one buffer at a stride
of N/2 + 48 values. Arrays of 8 KB allocated one after the other, or on 16 KB pages, start on the same sets of the L1
cache of the Apple M3 (128 KB, 8 ways, lines of 128 B): the 20 to 26 streams of the kernel evicted each other, and the
kernel alone ran at 43 µs per coordinate instead of 24 (`examples/ksrs.rs`, `results/rust/ksrs_mac2.txt`). Together,
1.05 to 1.14 times as fast on the M3 Max (`results/rust/comb_kab_mac2.txt`: modes 0u, 0 and 3, the latter now mode 1);
the outputs of the padded mode 0 are bit for bit those of the first version, mode 1 sums in another order (same noise,
`results/rust/comb_noise_ks1_cloud.txt`). Modes 2 to 4 (`kern::ks_src`): the same order, the digits and the FFT of b of
4, 2 or 8 values held in registers for the B - 1 automorphisms (14 loads per value and automorphism instead of 20 at
ℓ = 2, bit-identical to mode 1); default `KS_AUTO`: mode 2 up to ℓ = 2 (2 and 4 bits, 1.05 and 1.04 times as fast as
mode 1), mode 1 beyond (`results/rust/comb_kab_mac3.txt`). Stair key switch: the reduction of the inverse transforms
modulo 2^64 without the conversion to i128 (`rd::wrap64`, bit-identical), and the keys split re / im
(`examples/stairb.rs`: about 15% faster on the cloud VM). Configurations (`COMB_CONFIGS`) are those of `julia/benchmarks/comb_sizes.jl` (2, 4, 8, 12, 16 bits; p = 256
or 512).

    cargo run --release --bin comb_noise     # noise of CombBoot (NE errors, default 1600) against the prediction, margins
                                             # (MT=t: version on t threads)
    cargo run --release --bin comb_prof      # time of each phase (BITS=8)
    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin comb_ab       # A/B: CombBoot m = 1 / m = 2 (COMBM=2: m = 2 in the
                                             # other binaries; default m = 1, faster on the Apple M3 Max up to 12 bits)
    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin comb_kab      # A/B: orders of the key switchings and padding
                                             # (MODES=0u,0,1: by automorphism unpadded / padded, by source block)
    cargo run --release --example ksrs       # kernel alone: separate, contiguous (8 KB apart) and padded arrays
    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin rns_curves    # RNS: a 64-bit integer as 8 residues (moduli 263,
                                             # 262, 261, 259, 257, 253, 251, 247; COMB_RNS, B = 8, p = 512, from
                                             # the noise model), refreshed by one CombBoot (refresh_rns: test
                                             # polynomial and rounding resolution p p̂_k per column), CRT check, noise,

    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin rns_arith     # RNS arithmetic on 64-bit integers: refresh (CombBoot),

On x86, compile with `RUSTFLAGS="-C target-cpu=native"` (AVX2/FMA for the products in the Fourier domain); on Apple
silicon, NEON and FMA are the baseline and no flag is needed.

## Product of two ciphertexts (`src/product.rs`)

The product of Section 6 of the paper, ported from `julia/src/fast_product.jl`: LWE -> RLWE, trace modulo the odd
q' = 2^64 - 1 (product by N^-1, then 11 automorphism key switchings), tensor product scaled by p̂, relinearization by
a key for s'^2 (gadget (2^10, 4)), extraction. The FFT of TFHE-rs returns exact integers when one factor is transformed
as integers (`forward_as_integer`) and the other as torus elements (`forward_as_torus`), the result being read
modulo 2^64 (`backward_as_torus`): the keys of the trace are split in two signed 32-bit limbs and recombined modulo q'
(2^64 ≡ 1), the tensor product is computed in signed 16-bit limbs, so that the trace and the tensor product are exact;
only the relinearization (64-bit keys) carries the error of the FFT.

Configurations P (`PCONFIGS_RS2`, default, m = 2; `PCFG=rs`: `PCONFIGS_RS`, m = 1; k = 1..4): the refresh before a
product and the gadget of the trace, chosen by the noise model with the exact law of the
product noise (Laplace main term, failure 2^-128 for the refresh that follows, standard deviations × 2^0.2). Beyond k = 4 the product is not possible at
N = 2048 in double precision.

    cargo run --release --bin product_check                            # exactness of the trace, products at p̂ = 4, 16, 256
    RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 cargo run --release --bin product_curves       # times, one thread, same session, and noise

`product_curves` prints, for k = 1..4, the refresh before a product, the product itself (two traces, tensor product,
relinearization) and their sum, then the noise of NPROD products (default 200) against the prediction (inputs, trace,
product, key switch), and `&a * &b` on `FheUint2k` by TFHE-rs for k = 1..8 (TKS=...). Margins under the exact law: computed from these variances by the noise model, not included here.

## Multi-threaded versions (`src/mt.rs`)

Workers alive for the whole bootstrap, synchronized by a spin barrier (a fraction of a microsecond), each writing
disjoint ranges of shared buffers.

* `CombKeys::combboot_mt` / `refresh_mt`: per iteration, digits (coefficients split) | forward FFTs and half table
  (jobs) | key switchings and public products (destination blocks split: no reduction) | backward FFTs | CMux by
  `mt::ext_prod_mt` (digits, 2ℓ FFTs, products split by frequencies, 2 backward FFTs). Key switches and roundings of
  the B ciphertexts split by ciphertexts.
* `EbKeys::rotate_mt` / `refresh_mt`: the blind rotation of TFHE-rs (acc = X^-b v; acc += ExtProd(GGSW(s_i),
  X^a_i acc - acc)) with `mt::ext_prod_mt` (signed decomposition of TFHE-rs, SIMD of pulp as its `update_with_fmadd`).

Both give the same ciphertexts, bit for bit, as the single-thread versions (`mt_check`), whatever the number of threads.

    cargo run --release --bin mt_check       # MT against single-thread (THREADS=1,2,3)
    cargo run --release --bin comb_mt        # CombBoot: single-thread and t threads (THREADS=1,2,4,8; MTPROF=1: phases)
    cargo run --release --bin mt_curves      # T threads (THREADS=8), same session: latency of one ciphertext (TFHE-rs
                                             # classic and multi-bit GROUP_4, error bootstrap on 1 and t threads) and
                                             # one CombBoot call on T threads

Results: `results/rust/`. The secret keys of TFHE-rs come from its CSPRNG; the keys of the stair key switch and the
choices of the selection use `rand::StdRng` (benchmark code, not for production).
